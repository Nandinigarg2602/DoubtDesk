01 // THE CHALLENGE

The Doubt Resolution Bottleneck in EdTech

Why traditional discussion forums and issue trackers fail bootcamp learners.

⏳

Queue Stagnation

Students get stuck on runtime bugs (CORS, async lifecycle, missing tokens) and wait hours for an on-call mentor, causing friction and dropouts.

🔄

Repetitive Mentor Labor

Senior mentors repeatedly diagnose the same fundamental mistakes (e.g., useEffect re-render loops) 50+ times per student cohort.

⚠️

Premature Ticket Closures

Standard systems let mentors unilaterally mark issues as "Resolved" without verifying if the student actually understood the solution.

08 // INNOVATION 5

Student Quality Assurance & Resolution Sign-Off

Students have final verification authority before any ticket can close.

1. Mentor Proposes Solution

Mentors submit code guidance and click "Propose Resolution". The backend strictly blocks mentors from unilaterally marking tickets as Resolved.

2. Student Sign-Off (Accept / Reject)

• Accept: Rates 1–5 stars $\to$ Marked Resolved & indexed to FAQ.
• Reject: Submits clarification feedback $\to$ Ticket remains In Progress.
